# Project Name: Smart Cafeteria Ordering System

## Problem Statement
Students experience long waiting times at the campus cafeteria, especially during peak hours such as lunch breaks. This leads to wasted time, frustration, overcrowding, and, in some cases, students missing classes or settling for less desirable food options due to long queues or items running out.

## Target Users
- University students (on-campus and off-campus)
- Cafeteria staff and management

## Unique Value Proposition
A digital cafeteria ordering system that allows students to pre-order meals, select pickup or delivery options, and avoid long queues. The system improves efficiency, reduces congestion, and enhances the overall dining experience on campus.

## Key Features
- User registration and login system
- Digital menu with available meals and pricing
- Online ordering system (add to cart, checkout)
- Option for pickup or campus delivery
- Time-slot-based ordering to reduce congestion
- Order status tracking (e.g., preparing, ready, delivered)
- QR code or order ID for quick pickup verification
- Notifications when the order is ready or on the way

## Success Metrics
- Reduction in average waiting time at the cafeteria
- Number of students using the application
- Number of orders placed through the system
- User satisfaction and feedback ratings
- Improved efficiency in order handling by cafeteria staff

## Moore’s Vision Statement

**For:** University students who frequently use campus cafeteria services  
**Who:** Experience long queues, delays, and inefficiencies when purchasing food  
**The:** Smart Cafeteria Ordering System  
**Is a:** Web/mobile-based food ordering and queue management platform  
**That:** Allows students to pre-order meals, choose delivery or pickup options, and avoid long waiting times  

**Unlike:** Traditional cafeteria systems where students must physically stand in long queues to place and collect orders  

**Our product:** Provides a streamlined, efficient, and user-friendly platform that reduces congestion, saves time, and improves the overall food ordering experience through features like time-slot scheduling, real-time order tracking, and digital order management.
