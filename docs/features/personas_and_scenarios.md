### **Persona 1: Lihle (Student)**  

- **Age:** 20  

- **Occupation**: University Student  

- **Goal:** To obtain food quickly during short breaks without waiting in long queues
Scenario

Lihle is a university student who attends classes on campus and commutes from off-campus to her residence. She often has limited time between lectures and needs to catch a shuttle after her classes.

Due to long queues at the cafeteria, she struggles to purchase food on time and risks missing her transport. To address this, she uses the cafeteria application on her mobile device before her break begins.

She browses the available menu, selects her preferred meal, and places an order in advance. When her break starts, she proceeds directly to the cafeteria, collects her prepared order without waiting in line, and is able to eat and reach her shuttle on time.

### **Persona 2: Mr. Dlamini (Cafeteria Staff)**  

- **Age:** 40  

- **Occupation**: Cafeteria Worker  

- **Goal:** To efficiently manage food preparation and reduce congestion at the service counter
Scenario

Mr. Dlamini works in a busy university cafeteria where large numbers of students arrive during short break periods, resulting in long queues and high pressure on staff.

By using the cafeteria system, he is able to view incoming orders placed by students before they arrive. He prepares meals in advance based on the order queue and organizes them according to collection times.

This reduces overcrowding at the counter, improves workflow efficiency, and allows him to serve students in a more structured and timely manner.

### **Persona 3: Ayanda (System Administrator)**  

- **Age:** 30  

- **Occupation**: System Administrator  

- **Goal:** To ensure the system operates efficiently and that all information remains accurate and up to date
Scenario

Ayanda is responsible for maintaining the cafeteria system and ensuring that it supports both students and staff effectively.

She updates the daily menu, adjusts item availability, and ensures that pricing information is accurate. She also monitors system activity to ensure that orders are processed correctly.

In case of any technical issues, she resolves them promptly to ensure that the system remains reliable and supports smooth cafeteria operations.
